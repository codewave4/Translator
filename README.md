## 💡 Overview

**Translator** is a browser-based translation tool built to make Persian-to-English (and vice versa) translation seamless, accurate, and completely private. It requires **no accounts, no subscriptions, and no setup** — open the link and start translating immediately.

For casual users, it delivers fast, unlimited translations using public engines. For power users and writers, it allows connecting personal AI keys for natural, context-aware prose.

👉 **Try it now:** [https://codewave4.github.io/Translator/](https://codewave4.github.io/Translator/)

---

## 🎯 How It Works

```text
 1. Type, Paste, or Speak
    └── Dictate via microphone or type text in 22+ languages

 2. Choose Your Engine
    ├── ⚡ Default Free Mode: Instant Google & MyMemory translation (no key needed)
    └── 🤖 Smart AI Mode: Connect your own Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or Mistral API key

 3. Listen, Copy, or Save
    └── Native text-to-speech pronunciation, alternative phrases, and offline history
```

## ✨ Key Capabilities

### ⚡ Instant & Free Translation
- Works out of the box with zero configuration.
- Powered by Google Translate & MyMemory endpoints.
- Automatic source language detection and one-click language swapping.

### 🧠 Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) AI
Enhance your translations with state-of-the-art AI models for nuanced tone and literary accuracy:
- **Google Gemini:** `gemini-2.0-flash`, `gemini-1.5-flash`, `gemini-1.5-pro` *(Free API available)*
- **Groq:** Ultra-fast inference with `LLaMA 3.3 70B` & `Mixtral 8x7B` *(Free tier available)*
- **OpenAI:** `GPT-4o`, `GPT-4o-mini`
- **Mistral AI:** `Mistral Small`, `Mistral Large`
- **OpenRouter:** Access hundreds of open-source models

### 🎙️ Speech & Pronunciation
- **Speech-to-Text (STT):** Dictate your sentences directly using your microphone.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Hear authentic pronunciation for both source and translated text.

### 📜 Offline History & Export
- Automatically stores past translations in your local browser storage.
- Search and bookmark important translations.
- Download records as **TXT**, **JSON**, or a packaged **ZIP** archive.

### 🎨 Cultural & Accessible Design
- Authentic Persian **Girih** geometric backdrop patterns.
- Full bilingual UI (**فارسی** & **English**) with seamless RTL / LTR layout shifting.
- Eye-safe light and dark modes.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy Guarantee

- **No Middleman Server:** Every network call travels straight from your browser to the translation provider.
- **Local Storage Only:** Your history and API keys are stored exclusively on your device (`localStorage`).
- **No Analytics:** No third-party trackers, cookies, or telemetry.
